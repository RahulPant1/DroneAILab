# 10 · Rings race: racing tips

**Goal:** fly through every hoop, in order, in the direction of its arrow, as fast as you can.

## How to play
1. In the **Fly** tab, pick a track under **🏁 Race**. The drone moves to the blue start pad.
2. Press **🛫 Take off** (or the **T** key). The clock starts now.
3. Fly through the **yellow** (glowing) hoop. It turns **green**, and the next one lights up.
4. After the last hoop, the clock stops. Beat your best time!
5. Press **↺ Race again** (or **Enter**) to try again.

Keys: **W/S** or **↑/↓** forward/back · **A/D** or **←/→** left/right · **Space/Shift** (or **R/F**) up/down · **Q/E** turn.

Shortcuts: **T** take off · **Enter** race again · **L** land · **P** pause.

## Tracks (easy first)
- **First flight:** 3 hoops in a straight line. Just hold W.
- **Up and down:** low, high, low, high. Use Space and Shift (or R and F).
- **Zig-zag:** left, right, left, right. Use A and D (or ← →) while holding W (or ↑).
- **Square loop:** around a square. Turn with Q so W keeps meaning "forward".

The computer's autopilot does them in about 5, 7, 8 and 9 seconds. Can you beat it?

## Tips (and the science behind them)
- **Lean to go.** A drone can only push up out of its propellers, so it tilts to move. Watch it lean forward when you press W.
- **Brake early.** When you let go of a key, the drone doesn't stop at once: it has to tilt backwards to slow down, so it keeps going for a bit. Let go a little before the hoop you need to turn at.
- **Aim at the hoop, not past it.** In the zig-zag, head straight for the next hoop as soon as you're through one.
- **Use the poles.** The thin line under each hoop shows where it is on the ground. That helps you judge how far away it is.
- **Try it in the wind.** Turn on 🌬 Wind: now the drone gets pushed sideways and you have to lean into it.

## Self-check
1. Why does the drone tilt when it flies forward?
2. You let go of W just as you reach a hoop where you must turn. Why do you miss the turn?
3. Why is the square loop easier if you turn with Q instead of only using W, A, S and D?

## Answers
1. Its propellers only push "up" (out of the top of the drone). Tilting points some of that push forward, and that's what moves it.
2. The drone is moving fast, and it needs time and distance to brake (it has to tilt backwards to slow down). Also, while you hold a key, the spot it's flying to runs up to 2 m ahead of it, so when you let go it still finishes the trip to that spot. Let go a little earlier.
3. W always means "the way the nose points". If you turn the nose to face the next hoop, W takes you straight there; otherwise you have to work out which key means "toward the hoop" every time.
