DroneLab's EKF (`backend/sim/ekf.py`, 12 error states) was run offline on those inputs and both were scored against the simulator's truth (the `SIM` log message). ArduPilot's code is not used: it runs as a separate program and we read its log. Generated by `python -m backend.sitl.ekf_replay --write` (log `00000112.BIN`).

## What this shows
- **GPS glitch:** EKF3 rejected the fixes for 5.0 s, our filter for 5.0 s (the same 5-sigma rule: EKF3's `XKF4.SP` vs our test ratio). Position error during it: EKF3 0.21 m, ours 0.05 m.
- **A sudden 2°/s gyro bias is a tuning question.** EKF3-like bias tuning (5e-5): 3.48 m off, bias 1.91°/s after 15 s; our default bias tuning (2e-4): 2.99 m off, bias 1.89°/s after 15 s; fast bias tuning (1e-3): 0.23 m off, bias 2.05°/s after 15 s; EKF3: 0.93 m off, bias 1.22°/s after 15 s. A filter that believes the bias can only change slowly takes a long time to accept a sudden one. EKF3 rode it out (about a metre off, never lost); our simpler filter got lost with EKF3-like bias tuning (5e-5) and with our default bias tuning (2e-4) and needed its reset rule (GPS velocity rejected for 5 s: jump to the GPS and widen the uncertainty). Faster bias tuning learns it in seconds, but in DroneLab's experiment 12 the same setting makes the estimate wander more in a GPS outage. Every setting is a trade-off; real autopilots are tuned for the sensors they carry.
- **During the lap** our filter was closer to the truth (0.07 m vs EKF3's 0.42 m). Don't read much into that: SITL's GPS has no horizontal noise, which rewards a filter that trusts GPS more. EKF3 assumes real GPS (0.5 m noise, drifting, late), estimates 24 states (accelerometer bias, magnetic field, wind), runs two copies and can switch between them: built not to be fooled on real hardware, not to win in a perfect simulator.

## How the replay works
- Inputs from AP_DAL's replay messages: `RISI` (delta angle / velocity each 400 Hz loop), `RGPJ` (GPS), `RBRI` (barometer), `RMGI` (magnetometer). ArduPilot's body axes (forward-right-down) and world (north-east-down) are converted to ours (forward-left-up, east-north-up).
- Our filter starts from the sensors alone (accelerometer = level, magnetometer + declination = heading, first GPS fix after EKF3's origin = position), with EKF3's default noise settings (`EK3_ACC_P_NSE` 0.35, `EK3_GYRO_P_NSE` 0.015, `EK3_POSNE_M_NSE` 0.5, `EK3_VELNE_M_NSE` 0.3, `EK3_ALT_M_NSE` 2.0) and the GPS's 0.1 s lag.
- Errors are measured at EKF3's 10 Hz log rate, against `SIM` (10 Hz, interpolated).
