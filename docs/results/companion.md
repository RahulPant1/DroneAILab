# Companion computer + ArduPilot: perception as a separate process

DroneLab's perception (`backend/companion/service.py`) runs as its own process on ArduCopter 4.7 SITL's SERIAL2 port, like a Jetson or Raspberry Pi wired to a flight controller. Measured on SITL's ground truth. Generated by `python -m backend.sitl.companion_flight --write`.

## Precision landing (camera -> LANDING_TARGET -> ArduPilot PLND_TYPE = 1)
ArduPilot was told to land 1.8 m from the pad, as a drifting GPS would.

| | Landed from the pad | Camera frames | Marker seen | LANDING_TARGET sent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Without the companion | 1.82 m | – | – | – |
| With the companion | 0.06 m | 581 | 310 | 310 |

## Obstacle avoidance (lidar -> OBSTACLE_DISTANCE -> ArduPilot BendyRuler, OA_TYPE = 1)
An AUTO mission straight to a point 24 m north, through a 10 m wide wall 11 m away that ArduPilot doesn't know about.

| | Through the wall? | Closest to the wall | Path length | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Without the companion | **yes** (a real drone would crash) | 0.0 m | 24.2 m | 12.5 s |
| With the companion | **no**: went round | 2.88 m | 69.8 m | 34.88 s |

Safety check seen on the way: with `PRX1_TYPE = 2` set, ArduPilot refuses to arm until the companion's lidar is sending (`PreArm: PRX1: No Data`).

What would change on a real drone: only the two simulated sensor drivers (`SimCamera`, `SimLidar`), which read SITL's truth. See docs/learn/23-companion-computer.md.
