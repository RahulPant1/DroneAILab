- What is really tested: ArduPilot itself (its controllers, modes and failsafes) and the parameter file, on an airframe with your build's weight, thrust margin and battery. The hover throttle is ArduPilot's own control loop finding it. Hover current and flight time are NOT an independent check: SITL's power model is set from the Builder's hover current. The real number comes from your first flight log (BAT.Curr at hover).
- It can't check your real hardware: motor direction and order, ESC calibration, vibration, compass interference. Do the props-off motor test, then read the first hover's log in the 🩺 log doctor.
